In this work, we performed viscous hydrodynamics simulations.
In reality, viscosity is effectively induced by magnetohydrodynamic
processes such as the magnetorotational instability and the resulting
dynamo (Balbus & Hawley 1998), and for a more realistic study,
we need to perform a magnetohydrodynamics simulation in three
spatial dimensions, although such a simulation is computationally

this work, we employed a simplified equation of state assuming an
idealized cooling process for the optical thin region. In reality, cool-
ing is determined by radiation. Thus, incorporating radiation effects
is also an important next step.
